Fewer inputs, with an accuracy tradeoff

The baseline remains the default because its validation macro F1 is higher. The GA optimizes a training-only objective; it does not guarantee a better future model.

Selected estimator inputs

Four of 15 channels retained (73.33% reduction). The full controller also needs S1_Temp: five channels in total.

Input reduction is not a measured hardware or energy saving.

How selection works

Binary chromosome → training-block fitness → tournament selection → crossover → mutation.

Fitness = pooled training macro F1 − 0.05 × selected / 15.

Three seeds, 16 candidates, 10 generations. Purged blocks within the training day assess interpolation, not forecasting.

GA evolution · best fitness by generation

Later-test class 1 is absent. Test balanced accuracy uses its three present classes. Previously examined data are reused; this is not a new blind test.

Explainable rules, continuous requests

Mamdani inference: minimum for AND and rule clipping, maximum for aggregation, then centroid defuzzification. An explicit gate sets lighting to exactly zero for zero occupancy.

Input membership functions

All 34 rules

Thresholds are illustrative project assumptions, not ventilation or health standards. Environmental inputs outside the fuzzy universe are clipped and disclosed in the simulator.

Energy reduction must be read beside service failure

Replay integrates only adjacent records ≤60 seconds apart within the same split. Gaps, embargo boundaries and final unknown durations are excluded.

What the estimate includes

60 W lighting + 40 W ventilation, scaled linearly by the requested percentage. Energy = watts × seconds ÷ 3,600,000.

The comparator runs both loads at full power over exactly the same eligible intervals.

What it cannot establish

No HVAC, standby, controller or sensor electricity is included. Actual fan curves, comfort, lux delivery and air exchange are unmeasured.

The true-label reference is a counterfactual using known occupancy; it is not a deployable model or optimal control.

On test AUTO replay, both models produce the same energy request because their remaining differences fall within the same fuzzy occupancy region. Zero lighting during occupied periods can make the apparent reduction misleading.

A seven-minute project defence

  1. Problem & dataset

    Estimate occupancy from room sensors, then demonstrate explainable energy requests. UCI provides occupancy, not electricity labels.

  2. Baseline & evaluation

    Show macro F1 and balanced accuracy. Explain why 95% test accuracy is misleading when almost all test rows are empty.

  3. GA selection

    Explain the binary mask and fitness penalty. Four inputs reduce complexity, while validation quality declines.

  4. Fuzzy reasoning

    Run Empty + dark, High occupancy + high CO₂ and Low occupancy + bright in manual mode. Expand the fired rules.

  5. Failure case & energy

    Load a recorded false-empty example, then show energy evidence and occupied zero-light hours.

  6. Conclusion

    The result is a reproducible academic prototype. Future work needs new rooms, stronger occupied recall, temporal safeguards and measured loads.
